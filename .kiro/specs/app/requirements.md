# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This document specifies the user-facing web app of Naqsha. The app composes the finished modules `src/codec`, `src/reader`, `src/simulator` and `src/exporter` into three screens: Weave, Keep and Scan. It also needs a working `src/archive`, which today exports no functions (`src/archive/index.ts` re-exports an empty `types.ts`), so this spec includes the archive CRUD that Keep depends on.

Build order: Weave first (the guaranteed demo, reachable without a camera or a card), then Keep, then Scan with photo upload. The printable sample card (Requirement 21) follows Scan, because it needs the Scan_Screen to be useful. Deployment and README media (Requirement 23) come last. Live camera, Hindi voice prompts and animation extras are marked CUT-LAST and are the first items to drop if time runs out.

The app serves a handloom weaver, a naqshband and a textile archivist, all with low to moderate digital literacy (see product.md). The interface is Hindi first with English available, uses large touch targets, minimal text, and works on a phone. Look and feel follows `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`.

Scope notes:

- The app reads the card profile at runtime from `public/profiles/demo-12x16.json`. Its values are ILLUSTRATIVE and UNVERIFIED (card-geometry.md), so no UI code may hard-code card dimensions.
- Requirements 21 to 23 were added after the first approval of Requirements 1 to 20. They cover the printable sample card, the Card_Profile status notice and the public static deployment. Requirements 1 to 20 keep their numbers and text.
- The reader returns a card grid and a pose only. It returns no per-hole confidence. The tap-to-correct overlay therefore derives hole positions from the profile and the pose.
- The app imports other modules only through their `index.ts` exports. Files in `scripts/` (for example `demo_pattern.ts`) are not importable by the app.

## Glossary

- **App**: The Naqsha browser application in `src/ui`, built with Vite and React.
- **Screen**: One of Weave, Keep or Scan, reachable from the App's top-level navigation.
- **Current_Pattern**: The Pattern held in App memory that Weave plays, Keep exports and saves, and Scan appends to.
- **Card_Profile**: The CardProfile loaded at runtime from a profile file; it defines hook rows, hook columns and card geometry.
- **Sample_Design**: A built-in naksha, generated by the App from the Card_Profile, that loads without camera or card. Three exist: diamond, border and floral.
- **Weave_Screen**: The Screen that plays the simulator on the Current_Pattern.
- **Keep_Screen**: The Screen that exports the Current_Pattern as a BMP and manages the saved designs.
- **Scan_Screen**: The Screen that reads cards from photos and collects them into the Current_Pattern.
- **Scan_Batch**: The ordered list of scanned cards, one per pick, collected on the Scan_Screen before being committed to the Current_Pattern.
- **Archive**: The `src/archive` module, a thin async wrapper over IndexedDB.
- **Saved_Design**: One Archive record holding the Pattern, the Card_Profile name, the optional metadata and a creation timestamp.
- **Metadata**: The optional text fields name, naqshband, weaver, place, date and notes.
- **Translation_File**: A per-language file holding all user-visible text. Two exist: `hi` and `en`.
- **Review_List**: The list of Hindi strings in the `hi` Translation_File that need review by a native Hindi speaker.
- **Read_Failure**: A `ReadFailure` returned by `readCard`. Its kinds are `invalid-profile`, `invalid-image`, `peg-holes-not-found`, `ambiguous-orientation`, `resolution-too-low` and `card-outside-image`.
- **Export_Failure**: An `ExportFailure` returned by `exportPatternBmp`. Its kinds are `invalid-profile`, `invalid-card`, `empty-pattern` and `invalid-options`.
- **Sim_Failure**: A `SimFailure` returned by the simulator. Its kinds are `invalid-profile`, `invalid-settings` and `pick-out-of-range`.
- **Touch_Target**: The tappable area of an interactive control.
- **Built_Site**: The static output of `npm run build` in `dist/`.
- **Initial_Load**: The JavaScript transferred and executed to show the first Screen on a cold cache.
- **Printable_Card**: A downloadable drawing of one card of a Sample_Design, generated from the Card_Profile, showing manila stock, punched holes, unpunched hook positions, peg holes and lacing holes. It is printed ink on paper and is not a cut card.
- **Print_Scale**: The ratio of the printed size of the Printable_Card to the card size in the Card_Profile (`cardWidthMm` by `cardHeightMm`). The value 100% means one millimetre in the Card_Profile prints as one millimetre on paper.
- **Printable_Card_Raster**: The Printable_Card drawn as pixel data (`PixelData`) by a pure function, at a stated number of pixels per millimetre, with no browser API.
- **Profile_Status**: The optional `status` text of the Card_Profile. The demo profile `public/profiles/demo-12x16.json` holds "ILLUSTRATIVE, UNVERIFIED, replace with measured values".
- **Unverified_Profile**: A Card_Profile whose Profile_Status text contains the word "UNVERIFIED".
- **Deploy_Host**: A static file hosting service (Vercel in the documented steps) that serves the Built_Site at a public URL.
- **README_Media**: The banner image and the GIF files stored in `media/` and referenced from `README.md`.
- **Service_Worker**: The script `sw.js` of the Built_Site, registered by the App, that holds a copy of the Built_Site's files and answers requests for them from that copy.
- **Precache**: The named cache the Service_Worker fills when it installs, holding one response per file of the Built_Site.
- **Web_Manifest**: The `manifest.webmanifest` file of the Built_Site, with the App's name, start address, colours and icons, that lets a browser install the App.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: App shell and navigation

**User Story:** As a weaver, I want three clearly named screens that I can reach from anywhere, so that I never get lost.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL provide the Weave_Screen, the Keep_Screen and the Scan_Screen.
2. WHEN the App starts, THE App SHALL show the Weave_Screen.
3. THE App SHALL show a navigation control for each of the three Screens on every Screen, as a visible control and not inside a menu.
4. WHEN the user selects a navigation control, THE App SHALL show the chosen Screen and keep the Current_Pattern unchanged.
5. THE App SHALL keep the Current_Pattern in memory for the lifetime of the page.
6. WHEN the App starts, THE App SHALL fetch the Card_Profile and validate it with `parseCardProfile`.
7. IF the Card_Profile cannot be fetched or fails validation, THEN THE App SHALL show a plain-language error naming the problem and SHALL keep the navigation usable.

### Requirement 2: Weave Screen playback

**User Story:** As a weaver, I want to play the cloth being woven pick by pick, so that I can see whether the cards make the cloth I intend.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Current_Pattern has at least one pick, THE Weave_Screen SHALL render the cloth through `prepareCloth`, `createPlayer` and `paintToCanvas`.
2. WHEN the user activates play, THE Weave_Screen SHALL play the Current_Pattern from the first pick at the selected speed.
3. WHEN the user activates pause, THE Weave_Screen SHALL stop playback and keep the cloth as woven so far.
4. WHEN the user activates play while paused, THE Weave_Screen SHALL resume from the paused frame.
5. WHEN the user activates step forward, THE Weave_Screen SHALL advance the cloth by exactly one pick.
6. WHEN the user activates step back, THE Weave_Screen SHALL move the cloth back by exactly one pick.
7. WHEN the user moves the scrubber to a pick, THE Weave_Screen SHALL show the cloth with picks 0 up to that pick woven.
8. THE Weave_Screen SHALL provide a playback speed control with at least three speeds.
9. WHILE playback reaches the last pick, THE Weave_Screen SHALL stop and show the finished cloth.
10. THE Weave_Screen SHALL show the current pick number and the total number of picks.
11. WHEN the Current_Pattern contains a missing or damaged card (a `GapReport` from `prepareCloth`), THE Weave_Screen SHALL show that pick as a hatched gap and SHALL list the affected pick numbers in text.
12. IF `prepareCloth`, `parseRenderSettings` or the player returns a Sim_Failure, THEN THE Weave_Screen SHALL show a plain-language message for that Sim_Failure kind and SHALL keep the previous cloth on screen.
13. WHILE the Current_Pattern is empty, THE Weave_Screen SHALL show the "Try it" entry and SHALL disable the playback controls.

### Requirement 3: Weave Screen cloth controls

**User Story:** As a weaver, I want to change thread colours, add zari and set the repeat, so that I can preview the cloth with my own threads.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Weave_Screen SHALL provide a warp colour control, offering a set of at least six named colours.
2. THE Weave_Screen SHALL provide a weft colour control, offering the same set of colours.
3. WHERE the zari option is on, THE Weave_Screen SHALL weave every second pick with a zari weft (`{ kind: 'zari', tone }`) and the remaining picks with the chosen plain weft.
4. WHERE the zari option is on, THE Weave_Screen SHALL offer the tones gold and silver.
5. THE Weave_Screen SHALL provide a repeat control with integer values from 1 to 8, with a default of 1.
6. WHEN the user changes a warp colour, weft colour, zari option or repeat, THE Weave_Screen SHALL rebuild the cloth with the new `RenderSettings` and keep the current pick.
7. THE Weave_Screen SHALL size the canvas to the available width of the Screen, within the viewport limits accepted by `parseRenderSettings` (1 to 4096 per side).
8. THE Weave_Screen SHALL hide the thread size, float highlight, picks per second number entry and all other `sim-preview.html` controls that are not listed in Requirements 2 and 3.

### Requirement 4: Try it sample designs

**User Story:** As a first-time visitor, I want to see cloth weaving within a minute without a camera or a card, so that I understand what Naqsha does.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Weave_Screen SHALL show a "Try it" entry with three Sample_Designs: a diamond, a border and a floral.
2. WHEN the user selects a Sample_Design, THE App SHALL replace the Current_Pattern with that design, generated for the Card_Profile, and start playback on the Weave_Screen without further input.
3. THE App SHALL generate each Sample_Design from the Card_Profile `hookRows` and `hookColumns`, with no hard-coded card dimensions.
4. FOR ALL valid Card_Profiles, each generated Sample_Design SHALL be a valid Pattern, that is, every card passes `validateCardGrid` for that Card_Profile.
5. THE App SHALL generate each Sample_Design as a naksha of at least two repeats of its motif along the picks, so that the motif is recognisable in the cloth.
6. THE App SHALL generate each Sample_Design as a pure function of the Card_Profile, so that the same Card_Profile always gives the same Pattern.
7. WHEN the user selects a Sample_Design while the Current_Pattern holds unsaved scanned cards, THE App SHALL ask for confirmation before replacing the Current_Pattern.
8. THE App SHALL load the Sample_Design and show the first woven pick within 2 seconds of selection on a mid-range 2020 smartphone, with no network request after Initial_Load other than the Card_Profile.

### Requirement 5: Export to BMP

**User Story:** As a naqshband, I want to export my design as a BMP for an electronic loom, so that I do not have to redraw it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Keep_Screen SHALL provide an export control labelled with the loom purpose ("For electronic loom (BMP)" and its Hindi equivalent).
2. THE Keep_Screen SHALL let the user choose the bit depth (1-bit or 8-bit), the lift colour (black or white), the pick order (first-at-top or first-at-bottom) and the end order (first-at-left or first-at-right).
3. THE Keep_Screen SHALL start with the values of `DEFAULT_EXPORT_OPTIONS`.
4. THE Keep_Screen SHALL describe the options "orientation" in plain words, using the pick order and end order choices and no more than one line of text per choice.
5. WHEN the user activates export, THE Keep_Screen SHALL call `exportPatternBmp` with the Card_Profile, the Current_Pattern and the chosen options, and SHALL pass the bytes to `downloadBmp` with the file name from `bmpFileName`.
6. WHILE the Current_Pattern is empty, THE Keep_Screen SHALL disable the export control and show a message naming how to get a design (Try it or Scan).
7. IF `exportPatternBmp` returns an Export_Failure, THEN THE Keep_Screen SHALL show a plain-language message for that Export_Failure kind and SHALL not start a download.
8. FOR ALL valid Card_Profiles, Patterns and options, `readPatternBmp` applied to the exported bytes with the same options SHALL return the original Pattern (round trip).
9. THE Keep_Screen SHALL state that no loom controller has been tested with the exported file.

### Requirement 6: Archive module

**User Story:** As a textile archivist, I want designs saved in the browser so that they survive a refresh and a cache clear.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Archive SHALL export `saveDesign`, `getDesign`, `listDesigns`, `deleteDesign` and the type `SavedDesign` from `src/archive/index.ts`, each async function returning a result object that names a failure instead of throwing.
2. WHEN `saveDesign` is called with a Pattern and Metadata, THE Archive SHALL store a Saved_Design in IndexedDB and return its identifier.
3. THE Archive SHALL store the Pattern, the Card_Profile name, the Metadata and the creation timestamp in each Saved_Design.
4. WHEN `getDesign` is called with an identifier of a stored Saved_Design, THE Archive SHALL return a Pattern equal to the stored one.
5. WHEN `listDesigns` is called, THE Archive SHALL return summaries ordered by creation timestamp, newest first, without loading the full Pattern of each.
6. WHEN `deleteDesign` is called with an identifier, THE Archive SHALL remove that Saved_Design.
7. IF `getDesign` or `deleteDesign` is called with an identifier that does not exist, THEN THE Archive SHALL return a failure of kind `not-found`.
8. IF IndexedDB is unavailable or a write fails, THEN THE Archive SHALL return a failure of kind `storage-unavailable` or `write-failed` with no partial record.
9. THE Archive SHALL import no other `src/` module except types from `src/codec`, and SHALL accept an injected database dependency so tests use a fake and not a mock.
10. FOR ALL Patterns and Metadata, saving and then getting a Saved_Design SHALL return an equal Pattern and equal Metadata (round trip).
11. THE Archive SHALL store data in IndexedDB only and SHALL not use localStorage.

### Requirement 7: Keep Screen saving

**User Story:** As a textile archivist, I want to name and save a design with details about who made it, so that I can find it again.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Keep_Screen SHALL provide the fields name, naqshband, weaver, place, date and notes, and every field SHALL be optional.
2. THE Keep_Screen SHALL require a non-empty Current_Pattern as the only condition for saving.
3. WHEN the user activates save, THE Keep_Screen SHALL call `saveDesign` with the Current_Pattern and the Metadata, and SHALL then show a confirmation that includes the saved name or, when the name is empty, the text "untitled" in the current language.
4. IF `saveDesign` returns a failure, THEN THE Keep_Screen SHALL show a plain-language message for that failure kind, and SHALL keep the entered Metadata in the form.
5. THE Keep_Screen SHALL show the text "This is a record of the design. It is not legal protection." and its Hindi equivalent next to the save control.
6. THE Keep_Screen SHALL list the Saved_Designs, showing name (or "untitled"), place, date and the number of picks for each.
7. WHEN the user opens a Saved_Design, THE App SHALL replace the Current_Pattern with that design and show the Weave_Screen.
8. WHEN the user requests deletion of a Saved_Design, THE Keep_Screen SHALL ask for confirmation, and WHEN the user confirms, THE Keep_Screen SHALL call `deleteDesign` and remove the entry from the list.
9. WHILE the Archive holds no Saved_Design, THE Keep_Screen SHALL show an empty-list message.
10. THE Keep_Screen SHALL render Metadata as text and SHALL not interpret it as markup.

### Requirement 8: Scan from a photo

**User Story:** As a card maker, I want to read a punch card from a photo I choose, so that I can digitise it without special hardware.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Scan_Screen SHALL provide a control to choose an image file.
2. WHEN the user chooses an image file, THE Scan_Screen SHALL load it with `loadPixelData` and read it with `readCard` using the Card_Profile.
3. WHEN `readCard` succeeds, THE Scan_Screen SHALL show the image with a marker at every hook position, using `cardToImage` and the Card_Profile hook positions.
4. THE Scan_Screen SHALL draw a punched hole and an unpunched hole with different shapes or fills and a text legend, so that colour is not the only signal.
5. WHILE `readCard` runs, THE Scan_Screen SHALL show a busy state and keep the controls responsive.
6. WHEN the chosen file is not an image the browser can decode, THE Scan_Screen SHALL show the message for the `invalid-image` failure.
7. THE Scan_Screen SHALL read cards with no network request.
8. THE Scan_Screen SHALL complete the read of a 1080p photo within 300 ms of detection time on a mid-range 2020 smartphone.

### Requirement 9: Read failure messages

**User Story:** As a weaver, I want to be told in plain words why a card could not be read and what to do, so that I can retake the photo.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF `readCard` returns a Read_Failure, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL show a plain-language message for its kind, and SHALL keep the Scan_Batch unchanged.
2. IF the Read_Failure kind is `peg-holes-not-found`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that the two large alignment holes were not found and to place the whole card in the photo on a plain background.
3. IF the Read_Failure kind is `ambiguous-orientation`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that the card direction could not be decided and to retake the photo with the card flat and lit evenly.
4. IF the Read_Failure kind is `resolution-too-low`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that the holes are too small in the photo and to move closer or use a larger photo.
5. IF the Read_Failure kind is `card-outside-image`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that part of the card is outside the photo and to move the phone back.
6. IF the Read_Failure kind is `invalid-image`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that the file could not be used as an image.
7. IF the Read_Failure kind is `invalid-profile`, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL tell the user that the card layout file is not valid, and SHALL list the violation locations in a details area.
8. THE App SHALL map each Read_Failure kind to one translation key, so that adding a kind without a message is a compile-time error.
9. THE Scan_Screen SHALL not show the raw kind string as the only message.

### Requirement 10: Tap to correct a hole

**User Story:** As a card maker, I want to tap a hole that was read wrongly and flip it, so that I can fix the card without scanning again.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user taps a hook position marker on a read card, THE Scan_Screen SHALL toggle that position between punched and unpunched.
2. THE Scan_Screen SHALL make each hook position marker tappable with a Touch_Target of at least 48 CSS pixels, using an enlarged hit area when the drawn hole is smaller.
3. WHEN the image is displayed at a scale where neighbouring Touch_Targets would overlap, THE Scan_Screen SHALL offer zoom so that Touch_Targets reach 48 CSS pixels, and SHALL not shrink them.
4. THE Scan_Screen SHALL mark each corrected position distinctly from uncorrected positions, with a shape or label.
5. THE Scan_Screen SHALL provide an undo control that reverts the most recent correction.
6. THE Scan_Screen SHALL keep the grid dimensions equal to `hookRows` by `hookColumns` after any correction, so that the corrected card passes `validateCardGrid`.
7. FOR ALL corrections, toggling the same position twice SHALL return the card to its previous grid (involution).
8. THE Scan_Screen SHALL make every hook position reachable and toggleable with the keyboard.

### Requirement 11: Collecting a Scan_Batch

**User Story:** As a card maker, I want to scan several cards in order and turn them into one pattern, so that a whole card chain becomes one design.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user accepts a read card, THE Scan_Screen SHALL append its grid to the Scan_Batch as the next pick.
2. THE Scan_Screen SHALL show the Scan_Batch as a list with one thumbnail per pick, numbered from 1 in the current language.
3. THE Scan_Screen SHALL let the user remove a card from the Scan_Batch, with confirmation.
4. THE Scan_Screen SHALL let the user move a card one position earlier or later in the Scan_Batch.
5. THE Scan_Screen SHALL let the user replace a card in the Scan_Batch by scanning it again.
6. WHEN the user activates "Done", THE App SHALL set the Current_Pattern to the Scan_Batch grids in order and show the Weave_Screen.
7. WHILE the Scan_Batch is empty, THE Scan_Screen SHALL disable "Done".
8. IF the Current_Pattern is non-empty when the user activates "Done", THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL ask whether to replace the Current_Pattern or append the Scan_Batch to it.
9. FOR ALL Scan_Batches, the Current_Pattern created by "Done" SHALL have as many picks as the Scan_Batch has cards, with card `i` of the Scan_Batch as pick `i` (order preserved).
10. WHEN a card is read but not accepted, THE Scan_Screen SHALL not alter the Scan_Batch.

### Requirement 12: Languages and text

**User Story:** As a Hindi-speaking weaver, I want the app in the browser's language when it is Hindi, and English otherwise, with English on request, so that I can use it without reading English.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL hold all user-visible text in the `hi` and `en` Translation_Files, with no user-visible string literal in components.
2. WHEN the App starts without a stored language choice, THE App SHALL use Hindi if `navigator.language` starts with "hi", and English otherwise.
3. THE App SHALL provide a language switch visible on every Screen, with a Touch_Target of at least 48 CSS pixels.
4. WHEN the user switches language, THE App SHALL change all visible text and the document `lang` attribute without reloading the page, and SHALL keep the Current_Pattern.
5. THE App SHALL store the language choice in browser storage and restore it on the next start.
6. FOR ALL translation keys, THE `en` Translation_File and the `hi` Translation_File SHALL contain the same set of keys.
7. IF a translation key is missing at runtime, THEN THE App SHALL show the `en` text and, when that is also missing, the key itself.
8. THE `hi` Translation_File SHALL use Devanagari script and SHALL avoid the words "bitmap", "matrix" and "decode" outside the cards-as-code view, using the weaver's words card, design and loom.
9. THE Review_List SHALL name every Hindi string that has not been reviewed by a native Hindi speaker, with its key, its Hindi text and its English text.
10. WHEN a key is added to the `hi` Translation_File, THE Review_List SHALL include that key until it is marked reviewed.
11. THE `hi` Translation_File SHALL be a machine-readable file whose reviewed state is held per key.
12. THE Review_List SHALL be available as a file in the repository.

### Requirement 13: Offline, no backend and build

**User Story:** As an archivist working in a workshop with poor connectivity, I want reading, weaving and exporting to work with no network.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Built_Site SHALL run reading, weaving, exporting and archiving with no network request after the Initial_Load, the Card_Profile fetch and the Service_Worker's one-off Precache of the Built_Site's own files (Requirement 24). (Amended in task 8.1: the Service_Worker fetches each file of the Built_Site once while it installs, so the earlier wording, which allowed no request after the Initial_Load and the Card_Profile fetch, was untrue. Every such request is for a file of the Built_Site and for no other origin, and no request of any kind is needed once the Precache is filled.)
2. THE App SHALL use no backend, no account, no API key and no secret.
3. WHEN `npm run build` completes, THE Built_Site SHALL work when served as static files (as by `npm run preview`) with all assets resolved by relative path.
4. THE App SHALL load the Card_Profile from a file inside the Built_Site.
5. THE App SHALL use font stacks with local fallbacks, so that all text is readable when web fonts cannot load.
6. WHERE web fonts are used, THE App SHALL serve them from the Built_Site and not from a third-party host.
7. THE App SHALL send no analytics or tracking request.

### Requirement 14: Accessibility, touch and motion

**User Story:** As a weaver using a basic phone, I want large controls, keyboard operation and calmer motion when I ask for it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL give every interactive control a Touch_Target of at least 48 CSS pixels in both dimensions.
2. THE App SHALL make every interactive control reachable and operable with the keyboard, in an order that follows the visual order.
3. THE App SHALL show a visible focus indicator on the focused control with a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against its neighbours.
4. THE App SHALL give every control and every image a text name in the current language.
5. THE App SHALL give the cloth canvas a text alternative that states the pick shown and the total picks.
6. THE App SHALL show every status (error, success, punched, unpunched, corrected, missing card, damaged card) with an icon, shape or word in addition to colour.
7. THE App SHALL give normal text a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background, and SHALL report any design brief colour pair that falls below WCAG AA with a proposed replacement.
8. WHILE the operating system reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, THE App SHALL turn off CSS transitions and animations, and THE Weave_Screen SHALL not start playback by itself.
9. WHILE `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active, WHEN the user selects a Sample_Design, THE Weave_Screen SHALL show the finished cloth and wait for the user to activate play.
10. THE App SHALL announce errors and confirmations to assistive technology through a live region.
11. THE App SHALL use a base text size of at least 18 CSS pixels on viewports narrower than 600 CSS pixels.
12. THE App SHALL lay out without horizontal page scrolling on viewports from 320 to 1920 CSS pixels wide.

### Requirement 15: Look and feel

**User Story:** As a weaver, I want the app to look like my own materials, so that it feels familiar.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App SHALL define the colour tokens of `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` for the Day loom theme as CSS custom properties.
2. THE App SHALL use Day loom as the default theme.
3. THE App SHALL use plain CSS Modules co-located with each component, with no CSS framework.
4. THE App SHALL left-align text, and SHALL place the main action of each Screen as a visible control.
5. THE App SHALL use no emoji as icons, and no purple-to-blue gradients.
6. THE App SHALL draw a punch card, wherever one appears, as manila stock with round holes, peg holes and lacing holes taken from the Card_Profile.
7. THE App SHALL show Keep errors in a form that states what went wrong and what the user can do next.

### Requirement 16: Budgets and module boundaries

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want the app to keep the module boundaries and performance budgets, so that it stays fast and maintainable.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Initial_Load of the Built_Site SHALL be less than 200 KB of JavaScript, gzipped.
2. THE App SHALL add no runtime dependency beyond `react` and `react-dom` without a recorded bundle size evaluation.
3. THE App SHALL import `src/codec`, `src/reader`, `src/simulator`, `src/exporter` and `src/archive` only through their `index.ts` files.
4. THE App code in `src/ui` SHALL import no file from `scripts/`.
5. THE App SHALL run cloth simulation of a 100-pick Pattern within 16 ms per painted frame.
6. THE App SHALL keep the module dependency direction of structure.md: `src/ui` imports other modules, and no other module imports `src/ui`.
7. WHEN `npm run build` runs, THE build SHALL complete `tsc --noEmit` with no errors, with `strict` mode on.
8. THE App SHALL contain no use of `any`.
9. THE App SHALL keep all browser API calls for camera, file choice and download inside the `src/ui` boundary or the modules that already isolate them.
10. THE App SHALL request and evaluate the Scan_Screen code only when the user first opens the Scan_Screen, so that Weave remains inside the Initial_Load budget. THE Service_Worker MAY fetch the Scan_Screen chunk into the Precache before that (R24.2); such a fetch SHALL not put the chunk in the Initial_Load and SHALL not evaluate it. (Amended in task 8.1: the Precache fetches that chunk while the Service_Worker installs, so the earlier wording, which said the Scan code loads only when the user first opens Scan, was untrue for fetching. The budget rule is about what the Initial_Load transfers and executes, which is unchanged.)

### Requirement 17: Testing

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want the app logic covered by property tests, so that failures point back to a requirement.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE App's pure functions (Sample_Design generation, weft plan building, Scan_Batch operations, grid correction, hole position mapping, translation key checks, failure-to-message mapping) SHALL each have example-based tests and at least one fast-check property in `tests/ui/`.
2. THE property tests SHALL take the Card_Profile as a generated or supplied parameter and SHALL not hard-code dimensions.
3. THE property tests SHALL be named with the requirement ID and a plain statement of the requirement.
4. THE tests SHALL use fakes for IndexedDB, the camera and the file picker, and not mocks.
5. THE tests for photo reading SHALL use the fixtures in `tests/fixtures/` with their recorded seeds and ground-truth JSON.
6. THE tests SHALL cover the four fixture conditions: clean, noisy, tilted, and noisy and tilted.
7. FOR ALL Card_Profiles and all Sample_Designs, the exported BMP read back with `readPatternBmp` SHALL equal the Sample_Design (round trip through exporter).

### Requirement 18: Live camera scan (CUT-LAST)

**User Story:** As a card maker, I want to point my camera at a card, so that I do not need to take a photo first.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE the page is served over HTTPS or from localhost and `getUserMedia` is available, THE Scan_Screen SHALL offer a live camera control separate from the photo upload control.
2. WHEN the user activates the live camera control, THE Scan_Screen SHALL request camera permission and show the camera stream with a card-shaped guide frame.
3. WHEN the user activates capture, THE Scan_Screen SHALL take one frame and read it with `readCard`, with the same result handling as a photo.
4. IF camera permission is refused or no camera exists, THEN THE Scan_Screen SHALL show a plain-language message and SHALL keep photo upload usable.
5. WHEN the user leaves the Scan_Screen, THE Scan_Screen SHALL stop all camera tracks.
6. WHERE the page is not served over HTTPS or from localhost, THE Scan_Screen SHALL hide the live camera control and show a one-line note that the camera needs a secure page.
7. THE live camera feature SHALL not change the behaviour of Requirements 8 to 11.

### Requirement 19: Hindi voice prompts (CUT-LAST)

**User Story:** As a weaver who reads slowly, I want the main instructions spoken in Hindi, so that I can follow them while my hands are busy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE the browser provides speech synthesis with a Hindi voice, THE App SHALL offer a control to speak the current instruction on the Scan_Screen in Hindi.
2. WHEN the user activates the speak control, THE App SHALL speak the instruction text from the `hi` Translation_File.
3. WHERE no Hindi voice is available, THE App SHALL hide the speak control.
4. THE App SHALL not speak automatically.
5. THE App SHALL work with no network, using only voices installed on the device.

### Requirement 20: Animation extras and Night loom theme (CUT-LAST)

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want a few extra touches that match the design brief, so that the demo feels finished.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE animation extras are enabled, THE App SHALL show detected holes with a short pop, staggered across the card, with a total of under 400 ms.
2. WHERE animation extras are enabled, THE App SHALL show the first weave of a design rising out of darkness once, with a skip control.
3. WHERE animation extras are enabled, THE App SHALL draw progress as a thread drawn across.
4. WHILE `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active, THE App SHALL show no animation extras.
5. WHERE the Night loom theme is offered, THE App SHALL provide a theme switch with a Touch_Target of at least 48 CSS pixels, using the Night loom tokens of `docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`.
6. THE animation extras and the Night loom theme SHALL not add to the Initial_Load beyond the 200 KB budget.

### Requirement 21: Printable sample card

**User Story:** As a first-time visitor with a printer and a phone, I want to print a sample card, photograph it and scan it, so that I can try the whole scan flow without owning a real punch card.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Weave_Screen "Try it" entry SHALL offer a download of a Printable_Card for each Sample_Design.
2. WHEN the user activates the download for a Sample_Design, THE App SHALL let the user choose which pick of that Sample_Design to print, defaulting to the first pick, and SHALL download one Printable_Card for that pick as a PNG file or an SVG file.
3. THE App SHALL draw the Printable_Card from the Card_Profile, using `cardWidthMm`, `cardHeightMm`, the hook positions, `pegHoles` and `lacingHoles`, with no hard-coded card dimensions.
4. THE App SHALL draw the Printable_Card as manila stock with a round mark at every punched hook position, a visibly different empty mark at every unpunched hook position, the peg holes and the lacing holes.
5. THE App SHALL draw the Printable_Card with the physical size of `cardWidthMm` by `cardHeightMm`, so that printing the file at Print_Scale 100% gives a card of the Card_Profile size.
6. THE App SHALL state the Print_Scale in the "Try it" entry in the current language, in the form "Print at 100% scale, do not fit to page", next to the download control.
7. WHERE the downloaded file is an SVG file, THE App SHALL set the SVG `width` and `height` in millimetres and the `viewBox` to the card size in millimetres.
8. WHERE the downloaded file is a PNG file, THE App SHALL write the pixel density of the file (the `pHYs` chunk) so that the PNG prints at the card size at Print_Scale 100%.
9. THE App SHALL describe the Printable_Card in the "Try it" entry as a print for photographing, and SHALL state that it is not a cut card and cannot be used on a loom.
10. THE App SHALL generate the Printable_Card with no network request and with no browser API outside the file download trigger and the PNG encoding call, which stay inside `src/ui`.
11. THE App SHALL provide a pure function that draws the Printable_Card as a Printable_Card_Raster for a Card_Profile, a CardGrid and a pixels-per-millimetre value.
12. THE Printable_Card and the Printable_Card_Raster SHALL be drawn by the same layout function, so that the geometry of the file and of the raster is identical.
13. WHEN `readCard` reads a Printable_Card_Raster of a CardGrid drawn at a pixels-per-millimetre value that gives a resolution accepted by the reader, THE reader SHALL return a grid equal to that CardGrid.
14. FOR ALL valid Card_Profiles and all CardGrids valid for that Card_Profile, reading the Printable_Card_Raster of the CardGrid with `readCard` SHALL return the same grid (round trip, draw then read).
15. FOR ALL Sample_Designs and all picks of each Sample_Design, reading the Printable_Card_Raster of that pick with `readCard` SHALL return the grid of that pick.
16. THE round-trip tests of criteria 14 and 15 SHALL take the Card_Profile as a generated or supplied parameter, SHALL use a fixed random seed, and SHALL not weaken the property by narrowing the generated grids or by filtering out failing grids.
17. IF a Printable_Card cannot be generated because the Card_Profile fails `parseCardProfile` or the CardGrid fails `validateCardGrid`, THEN THE App SHALL show a plain-language message and SHALL not start a download.
18. THE Printable_Card SHALL contain no data that marks it as a physical card for a loom controller, including no lift data in any file other than the drawing itself.
19. THE App SHALL generate the Printable_Card of a Sample_Design in under 500 ms on a mid-range 2020 smartphone.

### Requirement 22: Card_Profile status notice

**User Story:** As a textile archivist, I want to see which card layout the app uses and whether it was measured from a real card, so that I do not mistake demo values for real loom data.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Scan_Screen SHALL show the `name` of the Card_Profile in use.
2. THE Keep_Screen SHALL show the `name` of the Card_Profile in use.
3. WHILE the Card_Profile is an Unverified_Profile, THE Scan_Screen SHALL show a notice that the values of the Card_Profile are not measured from a real card.
4. WHILE the Card_Profile is an Unverified_Profile, THE Keep_Screen SHALL show a notice that the values of the Card_Profile are not measured from a real card.
5. WHILE the Card_Profile is an Unverified_Profile, THE notice SHALL include the Profile_Status text exactly as it is in the Card_Profile, in addition to the translated sentence in the current language.
6. WHILE the Card_Profile is an Unverified_Profile, THE Keep_Screen SHALL show the notice next to the export control and next to the save control.
7. WHILE the Card_Profile is an Unverified_Profile, THE Keep_Screen SHALL show the notice before the user activates export or save, and SHALL not block export or save.
8. WHERE the Card_Profile has no Profile_Status, THE Scan_Screen and THE Keep_Screen SHALL show the Card_Profile `name` and no notice.
9. THE App SHALL decide whether the notice is shown from the Card_Profile `status` field at runtime, and SHALL not hold the profile name or the status text as a string literal in a component.
10. THE App SHALL show the notice with an icon or a word in addition to colour, with normal text contrast of at least 4.5:1, and SHALL announce nothing through the live region when the notice appears on screen load.
11. WHEN the user switches language, THE App SHALL change the translated sentence of the notice and SHALL keep the Profile_Status text unchanged.
12. THE Saved_Design SHALL store the Card_Profile name (see Requirement 6), and WHEN the Keep_Screen lists a Saved_Design whose Card_Profile name differs from the Card_Profile in use, THE Keep_Screen SHALL show that name on the list entry.
13. THE function that decides whether the notice is shown SHALL be pure and SHALL have example-based tests and at least one fast-check property in `tests/ui/`, where the property states that the notice is shown if and only if the `status` text contains "UNVERIFIED".

### Requirement 23: Public static deployment and README media

**User Story:** As a maintainer showing Naqsha to reviewers, I want the built site hosted at a public URL with no login, and a README with a banner and GIFs, so that anyone can try the app and see what it does.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Built_Site SHALL be hostable as static files on a Deploy_Host at a public URL that needs no login, no account and no API key to open.
2. THE repository SHALL contain the Deploy_Host configuration file (`vercel.json`), if one is needed, with the build command `npm run build` and the output directory `dist`.
3. THE Built_Site SHALL load the Card_Profile, the translation data and all assets by relative path, so that the Built_Site works from the root of a Deploy_Host and from a sub-path.
4. THE `README.md` SHALL document the Vercel deployment steps: importing the repository, the framework preset Vite, the build command, the output directory and the resulting public URL.
5. THE `README.md` SHALL state the public URL once the Deploy_Host has published the Built_Site, and SHALL state that the site needs no login.
6. THE `README.md` SHALL document how to run `npm run build` and `npm run preview` to check the Built_Site locally before deployment.
7. WHEN the Built_Site is served by the Deploy_Host, THE Built_Site SHALL pass the checks of Requirement 13 (no backend, no third-party request, no analytics).
8. THE Built_Site SHALL serve the Card_Profile file with a response that is valid JSON for `parseCardProfile` when requested from the Deploy_Host.
9. THE repository SHALL contain README_Media in `media/`: one banner image and at least three GIF files, one for each of the Weave_Screen playback of a Sample_Design, the Printable_Card scan flow on the Scan_Screen with a tap correction, and the export on the Keep_Screen.
10. THE `README.md` SHALL reference the banner image at the top and each GIF next to the text that describes the matching flow.
11. THE README_Media SHALL be captured from the running Built_Site, using the demo Card_Profile, and SHALL show the Card_Profile status notice of Requirement 22 wherever the Scan_Screen or the Keep_Screen appears in a GIF.
12. THE README_Media SHALL each have a file size of at most 5 MB, and SHALL have alternative text in the `README.md` that states what the image or GIF shows.
13. THE README_Media SHALL not be part of the Built_Site and SHALL not add to the Initial_Load.
14. THE `README.md` SHALL state that the Card_Profile values are illustrative and unverified, and that no loom controller has been tested with the exported file.

### Requirement 24: Service worker, offline reload and installability

*Added in task 8.1. Requirements 1 to 23 keep their numbers and text, except R13.1 and R16.10, which were amended in that task because the Precache made their wording untrue.*

**User Story:** As a weaver in a workshop with no connection, I want the app to open again after I close it or reload the page, and to sit on my home screen like any other app, so that I do not need the network to work.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Built_Site SHALL register the Service_Worker, and THE dev server SHALL not register it.
2. WHEN the Service_Worker installs, THE Service_Worker SHALL fetch into the Precache `index.html`, the start address of its own scope, the entry chunk, every style sheet, the lazy Scan_Screen chunk, every other JavaScript chunk of the Built_Site, the Card_Profile file, the Web_Manifest and every icon the Web_Manifest names, and SHALL install nothing when any one of them cannot be fetched.
3. THE Precache name SHALL carry a version, and WHEN the Service_Worker activates, THE Service_Worker SHALL delete every cache whose name is not that version.
4. WHILE the device has no network, WHEN the user reloads the Built_Site, THE App SHALL be served from the Precache and SHALL show the Weave_Screen, play a Sample_Design, open the Scan_Screen for the first time and export a BMP.
5. WHEN a new Service_Worker is installed and waiting, THE App SHALL tell the user that a newer version is ready and SHALL offer a control that makes the new Service_Worker take over and loads the page again exactly once, so that no visitor stays on stale files; THE App SHALL not reload the page by itself when a Service_Worker first takes control of a page.
6. THE Precache list SHALL be generated from `dist/` after the bundler runs, so that no hashed file name is written by hand, and THE Precache name SHALL change when any precached file's content changes.
7. THE Built_Site SHALL contain the Web_Manifest and its icons, SHALL reference them from `index.html` by relative paths, and THE Web_Manifest SHALL hold the App name, a short name, the start address `./`, a display mode, a theme colour and a background colour from the Day loom tokens, and an icon of 192 by 192 and one of 512 by 512 pixels.
8. THE Service_Worker SHALL resolve every Precache entry against its own scope and SHALL use no absolute path, so that the Built_Site works under `base: './'` from the root of a static host and from a sub-path.
